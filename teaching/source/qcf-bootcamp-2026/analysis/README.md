# BTC returns vs inflation surprises (quick-and-dirty, Aug 18 2026)

Tests the thesis "bitcoin is a bet against sovereign fiats controlling their
money supplies" at monthly frequency, 2014-09 to 2026-08 (n=144).

Data (snapshots in this folder, retrieved 2026-08-18):
- t5yie.csv  — FRED T5YIE, 5-year breakeven inflation (daily; last obs/month)
- cpi.csv    — FRED CPIAUCSL (monthly CPI level)
- btc_monthly.csv — Yahoo Finance BTC-USD monthly closes (regenerated by script)

Measures:
1. d(5y breakeven), monthly: market-based inflation NEWS.
2. CPI "statistical surprise": annualized MoM inflation minus trailing 12m
   mean; aligned both same-month and release-month (t+1). NOT a true
   consensus surprise (no free survey data) — upgrade path: Bloomberg
   consensus or Philly Fed SPF (quarterly).

Results (2026-08-18 run): all correlations ~0 (max |corr| = 0.17 pre-2020,
inside 2se); months with big downside surprises had HIGHER mean BTC returns
(+4.8%/mo) than big upside months (+2.9%/mo); Nov 2021-Dec 2022 CPI shock:
BTC -59%. Interpretation and caveats in the QCF deck slide.

Run: python3 btc_inflation_surprises.py
