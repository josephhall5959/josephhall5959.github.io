# Payment speed: Bitcoin vs Visa (2026-10-17, revised after review)

Same period for both: Visa fiscal 2025, October 1, 2024 to September 30, 2025 (365 days = 31,536,000 seconds).

| Network | Numerator | Rate | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Visa | 257.5 billion transactions processed on Visa's networks | 8,165 per second, about 8,000 | Visa FY2025 Form 10-K, operational highlights (sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1403161/000140316125000089/v-20250930.htm) |
| Bitcoin | 161,516,748 confirmed transactions (sum of 365 daily values) | 5.12 per second, about 5 | Blockchain.com "Confirmed Transactions Per Day", stored as `btc_n_transactions_blockchaincom.json` (api.blockchain.info/charts/n-transactions, fetched 2026-10-17) |

Both are averages of actual use, not capacity. Bitcoin runs near its ceiling and Visa far below its capacity, so the ratio understates the gap. Calendar 2025 Bitcoin total from the same file: 152.3 million (362 days in the series). Superseded: 153.7 million (Blockchair via Bitcoin.com), secondhand.
