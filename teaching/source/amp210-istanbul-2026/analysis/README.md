# Replication package: figures for "Why Hasn't Crypto Taken Over? And Will AI Change That?" (AMP210, Istanbul, Oct 23 2026)

    python3 make_all.py

Fully offline with `data/` populated. Figures are written to the deck folder; recompile `main.tex` with `tectonic --keep-logs main.tex` afterward. Scripts and snapshots for difficulty, stablecoin share, and the QR code are carried over from the QCF bootcamp package (`teaching-slides/qcf-bootcamp-2026/analysis`, snapshots of August 18, 2026).

| Figure | Slide | Data |
|---|---|---|
| fig_leading_zeros | (backup) Writing the Next Verse Now Takes About 20 Leading Zeros | `data/difficulty.json`; zeros = log16(D x 2^32) |
| fig_energy | Bitcoin and AI Both Draw Heavily on the World's Energy | Cambridge (2025); IEA (2025); ethereum.org (values in script) |
| fig_issuer_balance_sheet | A Stablecoin Issuer Operates a Bank-Like Balance Sheet | Tether BDO attestation Q2 2026 (from the QCF package) |
| fig_btc_supply | (backup) Issuance Halves Every Four Years Toward a 21 Million Cap | protocol issuance rule plus actual halving dates (no external data) |
| fig_btc_difficulty | A Higher Price Draws Miners, and the Puzzle Gets Harder | blockchain.info `difficulty` (`data/difficulty.json`); Yahoo Finance monthly BTC-USD (`data/btc_monthly.csv`) |
| fig_stablecoin_share | (backup) Crypto Payments Remain a Sliver of Cross-Border Retail | FXC Intelligence-Allium (2025), ~0.3% |
| fig_forum_scatter | AI Writing Weakened a $3.5 Billion DAO's Forum | Huang and Hall (2026) matched proposals (`data/matched_proposals.csv`, copied from ~/arbitrum-governance/data) |
| fig_qr_site | title, credits | josephphall.com |

Diagrams (hash chain, escrow, DAO timeline) are TikZ in `main.tex`.

Deck source: `main.tex` is generated by `../build_body.py` from `../preamble_v4.tex` and `../backup_v3.tex`; edit `build_body.py`, not `main.tex`. `../render_check.py OUTDIR` renders every frame for visual review.

**Public copy note (2026-10-23):** `data/matched_proposals.csv` (the matched Arbitrum forum proposals behind `fig_forum_scatter`) belongs to the Huang and Hall (2026) working paper and is not included here; the other figures reproduce offline from the snapshots in `data/`.
