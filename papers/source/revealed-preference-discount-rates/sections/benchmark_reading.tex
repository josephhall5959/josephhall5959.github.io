On the rate margin the published elasticities of default to the payment run from $0.03$ and $0.3$ in two card studies through $1.2$ to $2.5$ for hybrid-mortgage resets, HAMP payment cuts and the subprime auto rate, to $5$ to $6.4$ for resets on bankruptcy, installment changes and renewals, and the bureau's within-borrower estimate with the balance held fixed, $2.01$ ($0.02$) at twelve months and $1.99$ ($0.01$) at twenty-four, sits inside that range. On the paper's own margin the published record is thin and does not agree with itself, $1.7$ ($0.1$) for a subprime auto loan-size hazard, $1.2$ ($0.3$) for a HAMP payment cut that extends the maturity and $-1.0$ ($0.5$) for the Dobbie--Song rescheduling, and the bureau's within-borrower $0.44$ ($0.005$) and $0.53$ ($0.003$) lie between them. Among the population designs the cells with a score-by-income dimension come closest to the within-borrower estimate, $0.41$ and $0.37$ against $0.44$, with the one-percent panel at $0.45$, while designs with place or lender cells alone give $0.17$ to $0.25$, half the within-borrower value; with the balance held fixed the population designs give $2.33$ to $2.42$ against $2.01$ within borrower, the excess being the cross-borrower selection in the rate a lender prices.
